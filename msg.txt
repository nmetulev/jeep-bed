Add ADRIAN licence plate; fix roof braces to real 2x4s in body colour

The two roof braces were declared mat 'body', which is not a key in
matColors() (the body colour lives under 'red'), so Three.js silently
fell back to bare white. They were also 3.5 x 4, which is neither a
2x4 nor a plywood strip. They are now 1 1/2 x 3 1/2 -- an ordinary
2x4 on edge -- set flush with the top of the roof rail and painted
body colour.

New: a 12 x 6 licence plate in 1/2" MDF bolted to the grill face,
cut from the grill-face offcut. The name is live-editable from the
control panel and renders in the 3D model (CanvasTexture) and in
Fig 2. Cut-list row, shopping-list entry and a build-guide step
come with it.
